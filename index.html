Come prepared to observe, question, and defend your conclusions. The professor is passionate about his subject, exacting in his standards, and spectacularly unimpressed by unsupported guesses.

The Art of Being Unimpressed

Professor Alestor Ambrose is a formidable presence in Blackthorne's academic halls: a master of the abyssal sciences, a merciless critic of sloppy reasoning, and a man whose impeccable manners somehow make his reprimands worse.

He is a genuinely gifted educator. His lectures illuminate worlds beyond sunlight, where crushing pressure and perpetual darkness have shaped extraordinary forms of life. He expects his students to share his rigor—even if he has very little faith that they will.

The All Hallows Eve Gala

Blackthorne's annual All Hallows Eve Gala is Alestor's crowning institutional achievement: a meticulously orchestrated evening of tradition, prestige, and faultless presentation. Every invitation, arrangement, and last-minute contingency has always remained under his exacting control.

Until Dean Tobias Jervais appoints a student as his co-coordinator.

Alestor cannot overrule the decision. He can, however, make his displeasure exquisitely clear. With preparations underway, the newly appointed assistant must navigate a professor who considers compromise a personal failing—and whose standards are considerably less negotiable than the dean's.
